HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Protect your house from 10 waves of zombies using chemistry. Your house starts with 1000 HP. If it reaches 0, you lose.

MOLE SYSTEM

You start with 100 moles. Every atom costs 1 mole. Clearing waves earns +20 moles. Spend wisely!

CREATING COMPOUNDS

Use the Atom Selector (bottom center) to pick elements and set subscripts. Click CREATE to add compounds to your inventory. Then click a compound in your inventory and click on the map to place it.

TEMPERATURE & PRESSURE

Use the top sliders to control global temperature (0-500K) and pressure (1-20 ATM). These affect zombie speed, compound behavior, and reactions.

HOUSE DEFENSE

Place compounds ON the house to create protective layers. NaCl heals +2 HP/s. H₂O creates ice shields at low temp. Layer substances strategically!

ZOMBIE TYPES

Basic: Slow, weak. Fast: Low HP, high speed. Fire: Strong in heat. Ice: Strong in cold. Toxic: Releases poison on death. Massive: Tank with devastating damage. Armored: Resists explosions. Electric: Electrifies puddles. Mutator: Adapts to repeated strategies.
